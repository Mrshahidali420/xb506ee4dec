# Overgeared, Episode 1 (English dub) - story notes

Names (real, dub spelling): Grid (game ID) = Takumi Nagamine, 25, Japan. Sister Sarah. Earl Asher (quest giver). Pogma (legendary blacksmith). Dorian (NPC guardian of House Elstine, also heard as "Doran"). Irene (kidnapped daughter). Yura the Blood Witch (level 230, model/streamer). Game: Satisfye. Place: Yatan Temple.

What happened:
- Grid, a level 73 warrior, is stuck on a secret SS-level quest. He pays 60,000 yen to identify an item: Pogma's tome (legendary class change).
- Greedy, he keeps the book instead of handing it in. The quest becomes "Earl Asher's Wrath". He opens the book, becomes Pogma's Successor (master blacksmith skills, Indomitability stat), then dies. Quest failure drops him to level -1.
- Game company executives note nobody else found this class and that his negative level gives him bonus stat points.
- Real life: Takumi is broke, his sister Sarah nags him, he watches Yura on a stream, remembers being bullied, nearly quits, then logs back in.
- In Yatan Temple (level 150 dungeon) Dorian drags him into a rescue of Irene. Yura attacks. Dorian makes a last stand. Grid dies, then survives on 1 HP thanks to the immortality bonus, but the temple collapses.
- He dies twice in 24 h: level -3, locked out 12 h. News calls him the Temple Wrecker. Dorian and his ring are gone.

Ended on: Takumi remembers he is a legendary blacksmith now and can craft a ring like Dorian's himself. Next episode: he starts crafting and gearing up.
Music: Kamin_EMIN_JONY. Page: anime (AnimeRewindRecap).
