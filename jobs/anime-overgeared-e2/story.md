# Overgeared, Episode 2 (English dub) - story notes

Names (same as episode 1): Grid (game ID) = Takumi, Yura the Blood Witch, Pogma (legendary blacksmith). New: Khan (old Winston Village smith whose forge is closing), the Merrow Company (thugs buying up materials), Ragus the holy fist and his party (test Grid, he refuses), Bonmir (best blacksmith in the game, only named in dub), Rabbit (killer sent at the end). Game: Satisfye.

What happened:
- Execs say Yura beat Grid and one is curious about him. Grid is now an enemy of the Yatan cult, level -3, no gold, thrown off a carriage for lack of 10 gold.
- He crafts a joke greatsword (shark, over 10 million stats) with his master creation skill. A high level party mocks his gear; he refuses Ragus's test.
- A village smith throws him out, then gives him a firewood chore. He unlocks Fortitude, hits level 1, the smith teaches him Yaffa arrows, and he makes an epic arrow (level 3).
- He sells the arrows on auction (92 silver each, over 10,000 yen, more than his daily pay). An archer in a guild finds one epic; the guild starts hunting the maker.
- Real life: he collapses from overwork, his parents worry, he earns 9,000 yen a day.
- At Winston Village, Khan's forge is closing because of Merrow thugs. Grid steps in, activates Legendary Blacksmith's Wrath, loses his weapon, borrows Khan's gear ("overgeared") and wipes out the gang. Quest cleared, level 21. Khan knows who made the weapons, then collapses.
- A man hears his mercenaries went missing and sends Rabbit.

Ended on: Rabbit is sent after the missing mercenaries (Grid). Next episode: Rabbit arrives, Khan's fate and Grid's next crafts.
