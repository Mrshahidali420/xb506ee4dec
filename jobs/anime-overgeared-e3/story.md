# Overgeared, Episode 3 (English dub) - story notes

Names (same as episodes 1-2): Grid (game ID) = Takumi, Khan (old Winston Village smith), Pogma (legendary blacksmith), the Merrow Company (boss Valmont, never shown by name), Rabbit (Merrow's polite fixer, an NPC). New: Hu-Roy (player, a "golden tongue" Rabbit hires, real name Dom Alun Batar), Euphemina the Duplicator (player, alias Irina, copy-skill epic class), the game execs (Quest B-408 meeting). Game: Satisfye.

What happened:
- Khan survives. He says his ancestor made two legendary weapons that only Pogma mastered; Grid claims he is the chosen successor. A multi-part legendary quest unlocks (Prove the Legend); Khan agrees to teach him.
- Rabbit hires Hu-Roy to talk the villagers into signing over the forge. Hu-Roy instead pretends to help them and gets a hidden quest, shares it with Grid, then tries to reach the Earl.
- Rabbit has him arrested. Hidden S-rank Quest B-408: survive 50 hours logged in. Execs say 9% chance; the game now treats Grid as his "Apostle of Justice" and Grid does not know.
- Rabbit corners Euphemina (she conned Merrow out of an advance as Irina) and forces her into a crafting match quest.
- Rabbit offers Grid a match: win and Merrow drops the forge and frees Hu-Roy. Khan volunteers Grid as Pogma's successor. Prize 600 gold (72,000 yen).
- Three days later in the arena Grid faces Euphemina; they trade insults and the match begins. Hu-Roy has under 11 hours left.

Ended on: the match has just started. Next episode: the crafting match, Grid vs Euphemina. Episode 3 ends at ~1316 s; the end song starts at ~1345 s.
