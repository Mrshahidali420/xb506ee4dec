# Overgeared, Episode 4 (English dub) - story notes

Names (same as episodes 1-3): Grid (game ID) = Takumi (real world), Khan (old Winston Village smith), Pogma (legendary blacksmith), the Merrow Company (boss in a red beret, never named in the script), Rabbit (Merrow's polite fixer), Hu-Roy (player, "golden tongue", trapped in the dungeon), Euphemina the Duplicator (epic class, alias Irina), Sir Leo (knight of Winston), the Morpheus execs. Game: Satisfye.

What happened:
- Dagger match, three hours. Euphemina fails to read Grid's skills, has Khan added to the stage and copies his master smith skill. She forges an epic dagger first.
- Grid's Minotaur-horn dagger turns out better, but Rabbit's guards storm the arena, say Hu-Roy confessed, and arrest Grid and Khan. Rabbit prepared it before the match.
- In the cell Grid's quest becomes Apostle of Justice (S-rank): free Hu-Roy within 7 hours or lose 2 levels. Merrow hides Grid's dagger and Euphemina takes the win. Rabbit makes her a private proposal. The villagers who rise up for Khan are arrested.
- Morpheus execs put Grid's odds under 1%, then bet on him. One hour left, Euphemina (cat hood) breaks Grid out for an orb.
- They fight through the dungeon; Grid beats Sir Leo, reaches level 45, clears the quest and becomes the first player with a second job (Hu-Roy becomes his Apostle partner).
- Hu-Roy stalls the guards; outside Euphemina wipes the Merrow army with Demon King Tailfire. Rabbit reports his boss, says Euphemina saved Grid at his request, and offers Grid a job at his new company. Grid agrees (orb still owed to Euphemina).
- News leaks: the game's first two-job player. Later Rabbit gets ten years in prison, so the company is dead. Khan says the Earl is sending his own daughter to run Winston Village.

Ended on: the Earl's daughter is on her way to the village. Next episode: Episode 5.
