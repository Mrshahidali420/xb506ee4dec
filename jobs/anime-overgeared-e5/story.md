# Overgeared, Episode 5 (English dub) - story notes

Names (same as episodes 1-4): Grid (game ID) = Takumi (real world), Khan (old Winston Village smith, Grid's master), Rabbit (Merrow's fixer, now in prison), Hu-Roy (player, black mage), Euphemina (girl in the cat hood; the Sadaka Guild hunts her), Steng (friendly player at the auction), the administrator (moustached man in the castle library), Lady Irene (the Earl's daughter, now in charge of Winston), Sir Phoenix (her knight), Takumi's mom, dad and sister Sarah. Game: Satisfye.

What happened:
- Takumi wants to quit his day job and make money in the game; his parents refuse. For a month he grinds blacksmithing at night; Rabbit's polite letter arrives from prison.
- In the capital a Sadaka Guild man stalks Euphemina in the cat hood; she beats his crew and their top assassin. The guild now hunts her.
- Grid's gear sells but is mostly normal; he rages at the devs. He earns 129,480 yen in a month, then pretends to go to work and plays all day from a capsule cafe.
- His best armor is only an epic. Khan sends him to the castle auction; only six items sell, none of his. The administrator buys his armor and gauntlets for 5,500 gold and orders three epic swords in one week.
- Crafting swords with Khan: three normals, a rare, two epics, then the last blade turns legendary. Title: The Only Legendary Crafter.
- Hu-Roy takes on an SS-rank quest (Yatan cult, Blood Witch).
- Lady Irene buys the legendary sword for 220,000 gold (26.4 million yen). Grid fantasizes (car, class reunion, crush Arisa), gets rare blue ore, a title and no taxes in Winston. The game staff say the sword will be worth hundreds of millions later.
- At home Dad's guaranteed loan has put the family 80 million yen in debt. Takumi shows 20 million yen from the game; Dad is proud. They are still over 50 million yen short.

Ended on: the family's debt, still over 50 million yen. Next episode: Episode 6.
