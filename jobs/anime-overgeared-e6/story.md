# Overgeared, Episode 6 (English dub) - story notes

Names (same as episodes 1-5): Grid (game ID) = Takumi (real world), Khan (old smith, Grid's master), Hu-Roy (player, black mage, Grid's clingy sidekick), Oliver (Khan's dead son), Pagma (legendary blacksmith, Khan's ancestor), Earl Stime, Sir Phoenix, the Yatan Church. Game: Satisfy.

What happened:
- After the legendary sword, Grid's stats jump, but the family is still 60 million yen in debt. He decides to make real money.
- Khan catches him using broken hammers and lends him a hammer (+10% rare craft rate). Grid is furious nobody told him sooner.
- Khan takes Grid to his son Oliver's grave (10th anniversary), tells Oliver Grid saved the forge, and offers Grid the forge. Grid promises to make its name known.
- Khan's ancestors appear as ghosts. Pagma's ghost gives the legendary job quest "Proof of Legend, Part 1": find Pagma's trail in Kasong Canyon. Oliver asks Grid to look after Khan. Khan wakes up thinking he dozed off.
- News: Yatan Church crushed Earl Stime's army; Phoenix barely survived; war quests everywhere.
- Hu-Roy returns healed and kneels to serve Grid; Grid tells him off. Kasong Canyon is level 160, Grid only level 45. He needs a sylphid scale (drops from frost orcs in the north), designs a fur armor set and goes hunting.
- Grid fights frost orcs, gains a skill from the title Apostle of Justice (earned by saving Uroi), grinds for days (80 orcs, 3 scales).
- Hu-Roy finds him; a level 140 frost orc chieftain spawns. Hu-Roy's partner title gives Grid +30% near him. Hu-Roy is knocked out; Grid gets the Legendary One title (immune to status effects) and fights on. Hu-Roy returns, uses Sacrifice for Justice, dies. Grid uses Unyielding Justice and kills the chieftain.

Ended on: the chieftain's death; credits from about 1330 s. Next episode: Episode 7 (the Yatan war is coming; Grid still needs Pagma's clue).
