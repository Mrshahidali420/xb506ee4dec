# Overgeared, Episode 7 (English dub) - story notes

Names (same as episodes 1-6): Grid (game ID) = Takumi (real world), Khan (old smith), Hu-Roy (dead sidekick, only mentioned in the source), Pagma (legendary blacksmith; dub subtitles spell it Pogma), Yatan army. New: Roy (rich rival with a big enhanced sword), Asmophel (red knight, the stranger's target), High Priest Malaxus.

What happened:
- Grid survived the boss raid with no sylphid scales but got blessed enhancement stones. Family still in debt, yet he blows 1000 gold on ten more enhancement stones and gambles his dagger up to +6.
- Roy, a rich player, mocks his dagger and the sword he bought; Grid says he made his own. Khan forged him epic armor.
- Grid enters Kasong Canyon (level 85, mobs 162), Khan's armor cuts damage, he kills a lizardman pack.
- A spider webs three players: Takumi's high school bullies. Grid mocks them for money, they refuse to beg, they log out. Grid feels worse.
- Grid fights the canyon spider, fuses two skills into Wind of Justice, reaches level 86.
- A ragged stranger offers an S-rank quest to condemn Asmophel (framed him with the Empress). Grid refuses; stranger lets him go.
- Grid finds Pagma's mural behind a waterfall, copies the dance for three hours, quest clears: Pagma's Sword Dance.
- Cliffhanger: Yatan army will reach the fortress in days; a knight blames High Priest Malaxus.

Ended on: Malaxus named (about 1325 s); credits from about 1330 s. Next: Episode 8.
