# A Silent Voice (movie, English dub): story notes

Names (real): Shoya Ishida, Shoko Nishimiya (deaf), Yuzuru (Shoko's little sister), Tomohiro Nagatsuka (first friend), Naoka Ueno, Miki Kawai, Miyoko Sahara (old classmates), Shimada (old friend, never named in the script).

Hook: Shoya sells everything he owns and goes to a bridge (suicide plan, told with care, no detail).
Sixth grade: Shoko joins the class, Shoya bullies her (hearing aids, notebook). Class turns on him, his mother pays the bill, Shoko leaves.
High school: Shoya sees blue X on every face. Learns sign language, repays his mother, plans to disappear after one apology. Returns the notebook, signs "friend". His mother stops him next morning.
Nagatsuka becomes his friend. Shoya and Shoko feed fish on a bridge, Yuzuru guards her. They gather old classmates, the amusement park day blows up, Kawai exposes the bullying, friends drift away.
Summer: birthday cake, fireworks. Shoko goes to a balcony to end her life, Shoya pulls her back and falls, coma. Shoko says sorry to everyone, Shoya wakes, bridge scene at night ("help me live").
Ending: school festival, Nagatsuka and Kawai welcome him, the X's fall away, he cries and finally sees everyone.
