# Children Who Chase Lost Voices (2011 movie), full recap, anime page (@AnimeRewindRecap)

Names (real): Asuna (girl), Shun (boy from Agartha who dies early), Shin (Shun's brother, cloaked, wants the Clavis back), Mr. Morisaki (teacher, wants his dead wife Lisa back), Mimi (Asuna's cat, really a Yadoriko guardian), Mana (village girl), Quetzal Coatl (ancient guardians), Amoroto (village), Agartha, the Clavis (crystal key), Alcangeli = just "soldiers".
Tone: dramatic, a little dry humor ("she turns into a tomato"). 83 segments, about 1,030 words, 12 chapters.

Story as told:
- Hook: Asuna goes to the underworld to save a boy and a dead woman's soul takes over her body.
- Flashback: lonely Asuna (dad dead, mom on night shifts) listens to a homemade radio on a hill, hears a strange song. A beast attacks on a railroad bridge; Shun saves her (hurt arm, train, jump off bridge). They bond over the song, he says he comes from Agartha, gives her a blessing ("I just want you to live"), then dies alone. Body found in the river.
- Teacher Mr. Morisaki tells the Izanagi myth, shows her the Quetzal Coatl book: Agartha can even bring back the dead.
- Mimi runs off; Asuna meets cloaked Shin (looks like Shun), soldiers want the Clavis, cave, guardian beast. Morisaki is with the soldiers: he only wants his wife back. Asuna goes with him; they sink into the ancient water and wake in Agartha.
- Morisaki dreams of dying wife Lisa; Shin gets sent after the Clavis; rescues Asuna from monsters, river; Morisaki finds them. Village Amoroto rejects surface people; elder says resurrection is forbidden; Mimi's duty is done.
- Soldiers chase them, Shin holds them; Morisaki and Asuna climb a giant cliff; Morisaki takes the Clavis, sends her home ("I want you to live", same as Shun). Asuna survives a night of red-eyed monsters, Shin finds her at dawn, they head for the end of the world, see a Quetzal Coatl die singing the radio song.
- Gate of Life and Death: Morisaki says "Lisa, come back to me", Lisa's soul enters Asuna, Shin fights to get her back, Lisa steps out for one last goodbye, Morisaki lets go. Asuna wakes up, goes home to her mom, ready to say hello to a world without Shun.

Where it ended: final line "She lost Shun, but she is finally ready to say hello to a world without him." Movie: no episode number, no "Follow for part 2".
3 orig lines (each under 3 s): "Asuna, I just want you to live." (1280.2), "My only goal is to bring my wife back to life." (2570.6), "I was just lonely." (5705.3).
