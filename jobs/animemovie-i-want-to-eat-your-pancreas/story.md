# I Want to Eat Your Pancreas (2018 movie, whole story, Anime Rewind)
Names (real): Sakura Yamauchi (dying classmate), Kyoko (her best friend), Takahiro (her ex, class rep). The boy narrator is called "the boy" until his name is revealed late: Haruki Shiga (spring tree). The friendly gum classmate is not named.
Hook: news reports the murder of 17-year-old Sakura Yamauchi, a stranger stabbed her. Then back to the hospital lobby.
Story: the boy finds Sakura's diary "Living with Dying" in a hospital lobby and learns she has a pancreatic disease. She makes him promise secrecy and drags him into her bucket list (barbecue organ meat, cafe Spring, a buffet, an overnight trip with truth or dare).
Kyoko is suspicious, Takahiro punches the boy in the rain, Sakura begs him to stay. She is hospitalised, asks what being alive means, hugs him, then sneaks him out to watch fireworks. He says he wants her to live, she promises to tell him before she dies.
She is released, he takes gum from his classmate, types "I want to eat your pancreas" at the cafe and sends it. That night the news says Sakura was stabbed by a random killer.
He hides ten days, then visits her mother, who gives him the diary. The last page is a letter to him ending with the same words. Her phone shows his message was read. He cries, says his name aloud.
He gives the diary to Kyoko, who rages and says she will never forgive him. A year later he and Kyoko visit Sakura's grave as friends.
Ended on: "Sakura is gone, but the boy she changed is finally learning how to live." (movie, no part 2; caption says nothing about a next part)
