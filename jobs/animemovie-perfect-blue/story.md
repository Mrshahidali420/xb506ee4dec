# Perfect Blue (1997, English dub), story notes

Names (real names, anime page): Mima Kirigoe (idol, later actress), Rumi (her manager, former idol), Tadokoro (agency boss, hurt by the letter bomb), Me-Mania (the stalker with the camcorder, runs the Mima's Room site), Double Bind (the murder drama Mima acts in; the writer and the director are killed), Cham (her idol trio).

What happens:
- Hook: Rumi, in a red idol dress, chases Mima through the night with a knife. Then rewind.
- Mima quits Cham at her last show, a brawl breaks out, a bloody-nosed stalker stares at her. Rumi opposes the move to acting.
- Fax with the word "traitor"; small part in Double Bind; a letter bomb hurts Tadokoro; Rumi sets up a computer and Mima finds the diary site Mima's Room, written in her voice.
- The script needs a brutal assault scene; Rumi objects, Mima accepts. She shoots it and feels empty. A pop-idol double (her old self) starts appearing and calls her filthy.
- A post on her site blames the writer; he is killed in a parking garage. Risky photo shoot; the idol double appears again. Me-Mania vows to protect the "real" Mima. The director is murdered too. Mima finds a bloody bag and doubts herself. Double Bind ends with a split-personality twist.
- Me-Mania corners Mima with a knife; she kills him in self defence. Rumi finds her and drives her home, then appears in the red idol dress and says she is the real Mima. She chases Mima through the night; Mima hits her, Rumi falls onto the road and is hit by a truck.
- Months later Mima visits Rumi in hospital (Rumi still thinks she is the idol). Mima drives away and says: "No, I'm the real thing."

Kept clean on purpose: the film has nudity and an assault scene; the script does not describe them in detail and no shots from those scenes (about 1900-2090 s, 2690-2740 s, 3425-3460 s) are used.
Ended on: Mima's last line in the car mirror (original audio). Page: anime (AnimeRewindRecap), Lordly_Feder, music_db 12. Source about 4662 s. No part 2 (single film).
