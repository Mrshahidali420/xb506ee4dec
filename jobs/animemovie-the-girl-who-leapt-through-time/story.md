# The Girl Who Leapt Through Time (movie, whole story, Anime Rewind)
Names: Makoto Kono (heroine, called Makoto), Chiaki (orange-haired best friend, secretly from the future), Kosuke (dark-haired best friend), Miyuki (little sister, only mentioned as "sister"), Auntie Witch (museum restorer aunt), Kaho (shy junior girl, not named in the script).
Hook: Makoto thrown off her bike toward a train. Then back to the morning.
Story: Makoto oversleeps, goes to school, plays catch with Chiaki and Kosuke. In the science lab she trips over a walnut-shaped device and collapses.
Her brakes fail on the way home, she is thrown toward a train, and she leaps back in time and survives. Auntie Witch tells her it is a time leap.
She tests it at the river, goes back a day for her pudding, then leaps for karaoke, dinner and baseball.
Chiaki asks her out, she leaps back to erase it. A number on her arm counts down her leaps (she does not know what it means).
Kosuke borrows her broken bike with a girl on the back. Chiaki asks if she has been time leaping. Her number is zero, the crash happens, then Chiaki uses his last leap to undo it.
Chiaki says he is from the future, came to see a painting, now cannot go home. He leaves, rumors spread, Makoto blames herself.
Her aunt tells her to go find him. She has one leap left, goes back to before the lab, takes the walnut to Chiaki, promises to save the painting.
He says he will wait in the future, she says she will come running. Ends with Makoto smiling at the sky.
Ended on: "Someday, she will run all the way to the future to find him." (movie, no part 2; caption says Follow for more movie recaps style, no episode number)
