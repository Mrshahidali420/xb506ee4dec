# Wolf Children (2012 movie), full recap, anime page (@AnimeRewindRecap)

Names (real, keep them): Hana (mother), Yuki (daughter), Ame (son), Sohei (Yuki's classmate), Nirasaki (grumpy old farmer), Sensei (the fox on the mountain). Father = "the wolf man" / "her husband", never named.

Story as told:
- Hook: Hana's husband, the last Honshu wolf, dies alone in a drain.
- Flashback: Hana, a poor student, meets him in a lecture hall, he reveals he is a wolf, she is not afraid. Yuki born in the snow, Ame the next spring in the rain. Same day he goes out and never returns; Hana finds him dead.
- Single mother raising two wolf kids in Tokyo: no doctor, neighbors complain, child welfare knocks. She moves them to an old house in the mountains. Rule: never turn into wolves in front of anyone.
- Farming fails, Nirasaki and the village help. Winter river accident: Ame nearly dies, Hana saves him.
- Yuki goes to school, hides her wolf side, bites Sohei's ear, he covers for her. Ame skips school and learns from the fox Sensei. The siblings fight (Yuki says she is human, Ame says he is a wolf).
- Storm day: Sensei is dying, Ame vanishes up the mountain, Hana searches and is knocked out. Yuki and Sohei stranded at school; Yuki tells Sohei "It was me", he already knew. Hana dreams of her husband, wakes to find Ame a full wolf. He howls at sunrise as the new guardian of the mountain; she lets him go.
- Ending: Yuki goes to boarding school, Hana lives alone in the old house, content.

Where it ended: final line "Her son guards the mountain now, and somewhere up there, he is watching." Caption has no episode number (movie).
