# Wolf Children (2012 movie), full recap, anime page (@AnimeRewindRecap)

Names (real, keep them): Hana (mother), Yuki (daughter), Ame (son), Sohei (Yuki's classmate), Nirasaki (grumpy old farmer), Sensei (the fox on the mountain). Father = "the wolf man" / "her husband", never named.
Tone: warm and emotional with small jokes ("damn fool", "free opinions", "Rude. But also, accurate."). 70 segments, about 1,030 words.

Story as told:
- Hook: Hana's husband, a wolf, is found dead in a drain on a rainy night.
- Flashback: poor student Hana notices a stranger sitting in on her lecture, shares her notes, they fall in love. He shows her he is a wolf; she is not scared. They live together; Yuki is born in a snowstorm, Ame the next spring on a rainy day. He goes out and never returns; Hana finds him dead, and hears him say "take good care of our children".
- Single mother of two wolf babies in Tokyo: no doctor or vet, neighbors complain, child welfare knocks. She asks the kids "people or wolves?" and moves them to an old house in the mountains. Rule: being wolf children is their secret.
- Her tomatoes die, Nirasaki calls her a damn fool but teaches her, the village pitches in, too many potatoes. Winter: kids race through the snow as wolves, Ame falls in the icy river, Hana pulls him out ("Mommy, you're squishing me"), he is fine and proud he caught a bird.
- Yuki starts school, hides her wolf side (bones, new dress). Ame meets a zoo-born wolf who cannot teach him. New boy Sohei says Yuki smells like a dog, she snaps and he ends up with a bloody ear; he tells everyone a wolf did it, keeps visiting, thinks wolves are cool.
- Ame skips school for the fox Sensei. Siblings fight at dinner (Yuki: human, Ame: wolf), wolf fight in the kitchen; Hana accepts they are choosing their own paths.
- Storm summer: Sensei dying, Ame wants to take his place, Hana begs him to stay. Storm day: school sends kids home, Ame vanishes, Hana runs into the storm in her yellow raincoat, is knocked out in the mud. Yuki and Sohei stranded at school; Sohei feels forgotten (mom has a new baby); Yuki tells him "It was me", ears pop out, he knew all along.
- Hana dreams of her husband (sorry, I watched over you), wakes to a full grown wolf Ame; he runs up the mountain, howls at sunrise as the new guardian; she smiles through tears and lets him go.
- Ending: Yuki leaves for boarding school, Hana lives alone, content, in the old house.

Where it ended: final line "She lives there alone, but happy, and her son guards the mountain now, always watching." Movie: no episode number, no "Follow for part 2" in the caption.
