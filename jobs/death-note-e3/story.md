# Death Note episode 3 (English dub), story notes

Names: Jack = Light (same as episodes 1-2), Ryuk, L (called "L" only), Jack's father = "the chief" / "his father", Jack's little sister (unnamed), the police task force (unnamed cops, one young cop = Matsuda, unnamed in the script).

What happens:
- Hook: the police task force walks out on the case, afraid Kira will kill them. Then rewind.
- The task force reviews tips (3,029 calls, 21 fake Kiras), the victims all died in the evening, so L and the police suspect a student. A young cop notes that violent crime has dropped.
- L asks whether the victims' faces were shown on TV.
- Jack helps his sister with homework, eats dinner with his tired father, and hacks his father's computer. He learns the police suspect a student.
- Jack and Ryuk go over the Death Note rules (cause of death first, details later).
- Prisoners die one every hour, on the hour (23 victims two days in a row). L reads it as a challenge: Kira can choose the time of death and has police inside information.
- Jack explains his plan: he left about 50 criminals alive on purpose. His real aim is to get close to L and kill him. L will investigate the police, the police will resent L, and the police will find L for him.
- Several detectives resign, saying Kira knows their names and faces and they value their lives.
- L brings in the FBI to watch the 141 police staff with access to the case.
- Ryuk tells Jack he is being followed by another human. Jack needs the stalker's name.
- Ryuk explains the two differences: a god of death gains lifespan from each kill and can never die; a god of death sees name and lifespan above every head.
- Deal: a human can have the Shinigami eyes for half of his remaining lifespan.

Where it ended: Ryuk says "Is it a deal?" after offering the eyes for half of Jack's life. Jack has not answered. The last line asks the question.
Next episode (4): Jack's answer to the deal, the stalker, and the hunt for his name. Clips run to 1278 s; credits start about 1290 s.
