# Death Note episode 4 (English dub), story notes

Names: Jack = Light (same as episodes 1-3), Ryuk, L, "the chief" (Jack's father), Ray Penber (FBI agent, the stalker; real name used), a girl on the date (unnamed), the gunman (unnamed, the drug addict from the news).

What happens:
- Hook: a bus gunman sees Ryuk, nobody else can, and he runs off the bus to his death. Then rewind.
- Jack turns down the Shinigami eyes deal (half his life) and says he wants to rule a perfect world for a long time. Ryuk only wanted him to know it existed.
- Jack jokes about wings, Ryuk says the Shinigami realm is rotting, Jack tells him to go home and fix it.
- Jack gets an idea to find the stalker's name. First he tests the Death Note on six prisoners: three possible tasks (bathroom, pentagram, letter) worked word for word, three impossible ones (Eiffel Tower, draw L's face, write a note about L) failed. Lesson: it cannot do the impossible, but controls anything a person could do.
- The police and L see the strange deaths. The chief orders them kept from the media.
- Jack picks the drug addict from the news, calls a girl, and goes on a date with Ryuk and the stalker following.
- On the bus the addict hijacks it. Jack passes a note torn from the Death Note. The gunman touches it and can see Ryuk, panics, empties his gun, flees the bus and is hit by a car at 11:45, as Jack wrote.
- Jack made the FBI agent show his badge, so he has the name Ray Penber.

Where it ended: Jack says "The Death Note never fails. You will be next, Ray Penber."
Next episode (5): Jack goes after Ray Penber and the FBI investigators. Clips run to 1279 s; ending credits start about 1286 s.
