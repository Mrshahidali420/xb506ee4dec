# Death Note episode 5 (English dub), story notes

Names: Jack = Light (same as episodes 1-4), Ryuk, L (the dub also says "Elle/El/Al"; we say L), "the chief" (Jack's father), Ray Penber (FBI agent), Naomi (Ray's fiancee, ex-agent), the FBI director, five task-force detectives (unnamed), Jack's mother and sister (unnamed).

What happens:
- Hook: Ray writes the names of his 11 colleagues and his boss; all twelve FBI agents in Japan die of heart attacks. Then rewind.
- After the bus, Ray shows his FBI badge and asks Jack to keep it secret; Jack promises.
- Ray tells fiancee Naomi about the hijacking; she finds the timing suspicious; he makes her promise to stay off the Kira case.
- Jack decides to wait a week before writing Ray's name. L gets another suicide note mentioning gods of death.
- A week later, Jack (hooded, in a station) tells Ryuk he found you can write the cause of death first and add the name after.
- Jack stops Ray: "I am Kira", kills a coffee-shop man to prove it, threatens Naomi and Ray's family. Ray gets an envelope with a transceiver, rides the Yamanote line, tells Jack there are 12 agents (4 teams of 3), writes names on pages torn from the Death Note.
- Ray steps off the train, cannot place the voice, dies on the platform. His boss and the other 11 agents die after receiving a file.
- The FBI director quits the investigation. The chief is angry at L for spying on the task force. Naomi learns Ray is dead.
- Family meeting: the chief tells Jack's family he leads the Kira case; refuses to quit. Jack promises to hunt Kira if his father dies.
- Task force: chief offers everyone a way out; five detectives stay (six with the chief). L messages, trusts them, agrees to meet at a hotel at midnight. Naomi questions the bus driver and thinks Kira rides that route.

Where it ended: the detectives who stayed let themselves into L's hotel room at midnight (door unlocked; one man quit just before). Video closes on Naomi closing in on Kira. Preview (1358 s): Naomi appears, Jack: "there's a god on my side after all".
Next episode (6): L meets the task force in person; Naomi closes in on Kira. Clips run to 1280 s; credits start about 1286 s; the preview at 1353+ s was used for one cliffhanger shot.
