# Death Note episode 7 (English dub), story notes

Names: Jack = Light (same as episodes 1-6), Ryuk, L (dub also says Elle/Al; we say L), Watari (L's old helper, first shown in person), Naomi (real name Naomi Misora; the dub says "Miserah"; alias "Shoko Maki"), Ray Penber (her dead fiance), the chief (Jack's father), the five task-force detectives (unnamed; the youngest loves the belts).

What happens:
- Hook: Jack checks his watch while Naomi walks away into the snow to die. Then rewind.
- Jack decides Naomi must die, plays along with her theory, learns she told nobody else, writes the cause (suicide) in the Death Note with the name she gave him ("Shoko Maki").
- At the hotel L tells the five detectives Kira is not among them, calls Watari, tells everyone to turn phones off.
- The countdown on Jack's watch runs out and nothing happens: the name was fake. Watari arrives with fake police IDs. Jack works out she used an alias (Ryuk saw the real name and kept laughing). She is careful because Ray died after showing his ID.
- Jack turns his phone off (he promised to let her talk to his father), rejects Ryuk's half-life eye deal, and Naomi says she will go to the police alone.
- L explains the fake IDs (Kira needs a name and face) and hands out belts with transmitters.
- Jack catches up with Naomi and says the task force hides its members, then claims to be a member himself. She says she was an FBI agent who worked under L and trusts only him; Jack offers to recommend her to the task force.
- She admits her name was fake and hands over her driver's license: Naomi Misora. Jack smiles, checks his watch; she asks why. She leaves, Jack says goodbye with her real name, and she walks off alone through the snow.

Where it ended: Naomi is gone (off screen, by suicide, as Jack wrote). Closing line is the L cliffhanger. Credits start about 1284 s; preview from 1350 s (one shot used, 1368 and 1373).
