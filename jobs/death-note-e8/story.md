# Death Note episode 8 (English dub), story notes

Names: Jack = Light (same as episodes 1-7), Ryuk, L, Watari, the chief (Jack's father, Yagami), Deputy Director Kitamura, the task-force detectives (Matsuda, Aizawa; unnamed in our script), Sayu (Jack's sister), his mom, Ray Penber (dead FBI agent), Naomi Misora (his fiancee, now missing; L fears Kira got to her).

What happens:
- Hook: Jack eats potato chips in front of 64 hidden cameras while two criminals die on the news. Then rewind.
- L replays the station footage of Ray's death: the envelope under his arm is gone, so Kira may have been on that train.
- Jack has written criminals' names days ahead as "insurance". L learns the name Naomi Misora (missing since the day after Ray died).
- L narrows suspects to the two men Ray tailed: the chief and Kitamura. He wants cameras and wiretaps in both houses; the task force objects (civil rights scandal), the chief agrees, even bathrooms.
- Jack checks his traps (paper in the door, handle lowered 5 mm, pencil lead on the hinge): someone was in his room. He talks to Ryuk outside, buys a surveillance book, tells Ryuk to stop eating apples and find every camera.
- Jack leaves magazines as a cover; the chief thinks it is normal but too obvious; L says "I do suspect him".
- L has Watari air a message at dinner (Interpol sends 1,500 investigators). Jack sees through it.
- Ryuk finds 64 cameras in Jack's room. Jack hides a tiny TV in a chip bag, writes names from the news while studying, two criminals die of heart attacks.
- L: Kira needs a name and face, so the Yagami family who never saw the news looks cleared, "almost too innocent".
- Morning: garbage day, the little TV goes out with the trash.

Where it ended: Jack smirks at the garbage truck. Cliffhanger: L's next move (episode 9 "Contact/Encounter", face to face). Credits start about 1283 s; preview 1350-1376 s (used 1366.5, 1367.5, 1371.3).
