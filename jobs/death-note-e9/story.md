# Death Note episode 9 (English dub), story notes

Names: Jack = Light (same as episodes 1-8), Ryuk, L (introduces himself with the alias Hideki Ryuga), the chief (Jack's father, Yagami), the task force (Matsuda etc., unnamed), Sayu (Jack's sister), his mom.

What happens:
- Hook: at the university entrance ceremony, L walks up to Jack and says he is L. Then rewind.
- Ryuk grumbles about no apples; Jack buys him some. Jack calls L: his family could not have known the two dead criminals, since they never saw the news. L agrees.
- Jack's trick: tiny TV in a chip bag, memorize every criminal's name from the news, write them on a scrap kept in his wallet and kill later, away from cameras.
- The task force finds nothing and removes the cameras and wiretaps. L says Kira may have kept killing without a flinch; a god would not need a name and face, so Kira is a childish human playing god. He wants to get close to Kira and make him confess.
- Ryuk reports all cameras gone. Jack plans to use his father to learn L's identity and kill him.
- Entrance exam: Jack bored, a weird student (L) is scolded for sitting oddly.
- Ceremony: Jack and "Hideki Ryuga" give the speech (both perfect scores). Ryuga, in jeans and sneakers, finds Jack after and says he is L, Kira's chance "just under 5%".
- Jack cannot kill him (maybe fake name, maybe not L, death would prove guilt). He plays along and smiles. L leaves in a limousine.
- At home Jack explodes ("Damn it! He got me!"), Ryuk suggests the Shinigami eyes, Jack refuses (killing a fake L would reveal him). He then decides it is a match of wits: friendly classmates on the surface, investigating each other underneath.

Where it ended: Jack's vow to kill L with his own hands. Cliffhanger: L's next move, a tennis match (episode 10 preview, 1345 s). Credits start about 1283 s; preview 1352-1376 s.
