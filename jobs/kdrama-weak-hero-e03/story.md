# Weak Hero Class 1, episode 3 (spoiler page)

Names (same as episode 2): Sam (quiet top student, grey hoodie), Max (red jacket, school tough guy), Bobby (glasses), Victor (the bully from episode 2), Rex (gangster, Victor's cousin, runs the rigged "Free Money" gambling site), Amy (runaway girl who lives under Rex, helps Max and Sam). Unnamed: a girl Sam walks home in the rain, the boy in the cast.

What happened:
- Hook: Max walks alone into Rex's house and ends up beaten on the floor.
- Rex vows payback and plans to make money. Sam beats Max and Bobby at pool, then meets a girl with no umbrella (tteokbokki, she saves his number as "Asshole").
- Victor begs Sam to lie about the fentanyl. Sam, Max and Bobby are led to a parking garage. Rex shows a boy in a cast and demands 15 million won in three days.
- Amy warns Max about Rex. Bobby sells his dad's watch for the money. Amy explains the rigged gambling site (loans, collateral, runaway kids forced to deal drugs). Ledger and drugs are at Rex's house.
- Sam's plan is to bring the police. Max goes first alone, pitches Rex a school gambling idea and gets in his car as the newbie. Amy gives Sam the address (blue door).
- Max talks to Sam through his watch, refuses to hand over his phone, gets beaten. Rex reads "coming with the police". Bobby arrives with the money and is pulled inside. The police have no warrant and leave.
- Sam sneaks in, finds blood and a safe room. Max's phone is unreachable. Max is out cold in a car with Bobby.

Ends: Sam alone inside Rex's house, Max and Bobby taken away. Episode 4 picks up from there.
