# Weak Hero Class 1, episode 4 (spoiler page)

Names (same as episodes 2-3): Sam (quiet top student, Si-eun), Max (red jacket, tough one), Bobby (glasses, grey hoodie, real name Beom-seok, son of the assemblyman), Victor (the bully from episode 2, Rex's cousin, Young-bin), Rex (flowered-shirt gangster, Gil-su), Amy (runaway girl with dyed hair, Yeong-i). New this episode: Jake (ponytail, tie-dye sweater, Rex's former man, real name Seok-dae), Tim (14-year-old boy in Rex's crew, Seong-chan). Unnamed: a man in a suit (the assemblyman's aide), Assemblyman Oh Jin-won (Bobby's father, never named in the script).

What happened:
- Hook: Rex pulls a knife on Sam and Max, and ends up flat on the ground.
- Rex's crew load Max and Bobby into a van and flee as police lights arrive. Rex calls Jake: come to Mangu-dong, bring Amy, or I sell this boy off.
- Jake leaves Amy to hide and goes alone. Amy calls Sam, who finds blood at Rex's house. Amy tells him to bring the police.
- Mangu-dong is an abandoned theme park. Max and Bobby sit tied up. Jake offers himself, Rex beats him, Amy is dragged away. Rex tells 14-year-old Tim minors don't go to prison.
- Jake challenges Rex one-on-one, wins at first, then Rex's men pile on. Amy pulls a knife, Rex chokes her. Sam walks in and bluffs that police are coming. Rex's men leave, return when they see it was a bluff.
- Brawl, then a real officer (Rex charms him). Rex flees, Sam chases, Rex pulls a knife. Max arrives; Rex beats Max on the ground, Max gets up and Rex ends flat out (the last hit is in very short shots; Max is the one standing over him). Rex is taken away by ambulance. Jake is taken by police.
- At the station Sam apologises to Bobby (his plan got Max hurt) and Bobby apologises back. A man in a suit says nothing will reach their families or school. He brings Bobby to a back room to hit Rex, who taunts him.
- Max in hospital with a broken arm: Bobby's family covered it up (Bobby's dad is the assemblyman), their names are removed, Rex arrested, a secretary offered to pay the bills. Amy and Sam tease about dating.
- Ends: the assemblyman on TV ("I am a father"), then in a dark room: "I shouldn't have taken you in. You are a bad seed." A boy stands in front of him in the dark, face unseen; the script ends asking if it is Bobby.

Ends: Victor's powerful father has buried the case and turns on someone in a dark room. Episode 5 picks up there. Correction 1 Nov 2026 (QA): Bobby is Beom-seok and the assemblyman is HIS father, not Victor's.

At the station the man in the suit (works for the assemblyman) buries the case and lets Bobby hit Rex. Final orig lines: "Just punch me", "How should I stab you?", "I shouldn't have taken you in".
