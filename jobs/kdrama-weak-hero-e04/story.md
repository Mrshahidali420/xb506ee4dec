# Weak Hero Class 1, episode 4 (spoiler page)

Names (same as episodes 2-3): Sam (quiet top student, Si-eun), Max (red jacket, tough one), Bobby (glasses), Victor (the bully, real name Beom-seok), Rex (flowered-shirt gangster, Gil-su), Amy (runaway girl with dyed hair, Yeong-i). New this episode: Jake (ponytail, tie-dye sweater, Rex's former man, real name Seok-dae), Tim (14-year-old boy in Rex's crew, Seong-chan). Unnamed: a man in a suit, Assemblyman Oh Jin-won (Victor's father).

What happened:
- Hook: Rex pulls a knife on Sam and Max, and ends up flat on the ground.
- Rex's crew load Max and Bobby into a van and flee as police lights arrive. Rex calls Jake: come to Mangu-dong, bring Amy, or I sell this boy off.
- Jake leaves Amy to hide and goes alone. Amy calls Sam, who finds blood at Rex's house. Amy tells him to bring the police.
- Mangu-dong is an abandoned theme park. Max and Bobby sit tied up. Jake offers himself, Rex beats him, Amy is dragged away. Rex tells 14-year-old Tim minors don't go to prison.
- Jake challenges Rex one-on-one, wins at first, then Rex's men pile on. Amy pulls a knife, Rex chokes her. Sam walks in and bluffs that police are coming. Rex's men leave, return when they see it was a bluff.
- Brawl, then a real officer (Rex charms him). Rex flees, Sam chases, Rex pulls a knife. Max arrives; Max and Sam knock Rex out. Rex is taken away by ambulance. Jake is taken by police.
- At the station a man in a suit says nothing will reach their families or school. He brings Bobby to a back room to hit Rex, who taunts him.
- Max in hospital with a broken arm: Victor's family covered it up, their names are removed, Rex arrested, a secretary offered to pay the bills. Amy and Sam tease about dating.
- Ends: the assemblyman on TV ("I am a father"), then in a dark room: "I shouldn't have taken you in. You are a bad seed." We never see who he talks to.

Ends: Victor's powerful father has buried the case and turns on someone in a dark room. Episode 5 picks up there.
