# Weak Hero Class 1, episode 5 (spoiler page)

Names (same as episodes 2-4): Sam (quiet top student, Si-eun, dark jacket), Max (Su-ho, blue hoodie / purple tee, the one who trains Sam), Bobby (glasses, Beom-seok, the assemblyman's adopted son), Amy (runaway girl, Yeong-i, stays at Max's place). New this episode: Dean (leader of Bobby's old bullies from Byuksan school, tracksuit jacket over a checked shirt). The other five bullies are unnamed. "Free Money gang" = the thugs from earlier episodes.

What happened:
- Hook: Bobby buys drinks for the gang that stomped him and holds up the cash for their photo.
- Back at school the three boys are heroes after beating the Free Money gang. Bobby pays for lunch. At night he scrolls Max's Instagram (grandma dinner, Sam's hamburgers) alone.
- Karaoke night: Bobby steps out for a drink and his old bullies spot him. They call his phone, Amy answers and Bobby snaps at her. The bullies walk in, call him "our little Bobby", and leave saying "answer when we call".
- Next day Bobby grabs a classmate by the collar for laughing. Sam pulls him off. Bobby's phone fills with missed calls (15, 16, 17); he ignores all.
- Max trains Sam by the river (chin tucked, takedown, "don't hit, run"). Sam and Max visit Bobby's mansion: "That's not my family", his father adopted him for his public image.
- Bobby tells them he was beaten at his old school until he was hospitalised. Sam says it's not his fault and gets him an apology.
- Bobby calls the gang himself; they go to the karaoke room. Dean says sorry, Bobby says "let me hit you once", hits him, brawl, Sam stops it: "don't call Bobby again".
- Outside Bobby screams at Sam ("you're smothering me"), storms off and turns his phone off.
- Alone, he walks into the gang again ("no glasses today?"). They drag him into a parking garage, check his money, stomp him.
- Then Bobby follows them to a club, says drinks are on him, holds up 500,000 won for a photo, follows them on Instagram, and unfollows Max. He ends drunk among strangers, near a table of girls.

Ends: Bobby has chosen the gang over his friends; Sam still cannot reach him. Episode 6 picks up there. Opinion lines used: "Easy advice to give", "That is what real friends do", "He's choosing the people who hurt him", "That hurt more than any stomping". Original lines: "That's not my family", "Let me hit you once", "You're rich, right?" (3 orig clips, each under 3 s).
