# Weak Hero Class 1, episode 7 (spoiler page)

Names (same as episodes 2-6): Sam (Si-eun, quiet top student, cast on his arm), Max (Su-ho, red/black delivery jacket over a red tee), Bobby (Beom-seok, the assemblyman's adopted son, black tee, bowl cut), Amy (Yeong-i, pink-tinted hair, works at a restaurant), Wolf (Woo-yeong, the fighter in the grey hoodie who films himself). Tom and Ben are not named this episode; Bobby's other friends wear white school uniforms.

What happened:
- Hook: Max lies in a boxing ring and a boy says he is not breathing.
- Back: Sam in the ambulance tells police he was alone, asks Amy never to tell Max. Max waits alone with a birthday cake. Sam calls Bobby: leave Max alone.
- Sam's parents fight; his dad says he is transferring schools; Sam asks since when he cared.
- Bobby's father, an assemblyman, has his aide cover it up and plans to ship Bobby to the Philippines.
- Max learns from Bobby's friends on the roof that Bobby used Amy as bait and paid someone to beat Sam. He visits Amy, then Sam (as a delivery boy) and says nothing.
- Bobby's father beats him. Bobby runs, a threatening message with Max's photo reaches him; Max grabs Bobby's friends to find him. Bobby calls Wolf: "Let's get Max, I'll pay."
- Max walks into the boxing gym, tells Bobby to apologize to Sam and Amy, Bobby refuses. Wolf fights Max on camera and Max ends on the mat. Bobby and his friends stomp him. Max stops breathing. The aide arrives and tells them to stay. Bobby begs Max to look at him.
- Sam and Amy search an empty garage for Max.

Ends: Max lies motionless in the ring. Episode 8 picks up there. Opinion lines: "Fair question, honestly", "That's what cowards always say", "Bobby just made the worst call", "Max never stood a chance". Original lines (3): "Stop this. Now. Leave Max alone", "Now who's the pathetic one?", "Max, please look at me".
Note: who wins the middle of the fight was read from stills only (Max drops Wolf once, then ends flat on the mat).
