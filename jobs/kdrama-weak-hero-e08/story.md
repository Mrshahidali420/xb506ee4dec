# Weak Hero Class 1, episode 8 (spoiler page)

Names (same as episodes 2-7): Sam (Si-eun, quiet top student, cast on his arm), Max (Su-ho, red/black delivery jacket), Bobby (Beom-seok, the assemblyman's son, bowl cut), Amy (Yeong-i, pink-tinted hair), Wolf (Woo-yeong, the fighter who films himself). New this episode, all unnamed in the script: the aide (Bobby's father's man in a suit), the athlete (Bobby's friend in the gym), the bully at Eunjang (smirking boy in a light-blue uniform).

What happened:
- Hook: Sam walks into an exam covered in blood, a fire extinguisher flies, Bobby's crew panics.
- Back: Sam alone remembers cleaning duty with Max. Aide takes charge of the ring scene: detectives are coming, here is the story (Max took a break alone and passed out). Max's phone texts Sam "working out, call you later". Max goes off on a stretcher.
- Bobby's father rages at him, aide to watch him until the plane to the Philippines.
- Sam sits exams, Amy tells him Max won't wake up; Bobby's people paid the bills and made Max's grandma sign a contract. The aide threatens Sam in the hallway: do as your father says if you want Max alive.
- Sam beats a classmate for answers, finds the athlete in the weight room (flashback of Max teaching him to guard his chin and to run), beats him; the athlete says Bobby kept kicking Max's head.
- Bobby's father visits the school and hears the "accident" story. Sam walks into the exam room bloody, a fire extinguisher hits him, he beats Bobby in front of everyone and cries in the hallway.
- Max wakes up in hospital; Sam, asleep at his bed, apologises ("I'm sorry", "Me too"). Bobby went abroad, Amy left after apologizing. Bobby's father spares Sam juvie only if he transfers and keeps quiet; Eunjang High is the only school that takes him.
- At Eunjang a bully mocks his name and calls him a slave. Sam says they are predictable. A tattooed hand opens a photo of him: "He beat Kang Woo-yeong, a Union candidate. What should we do?"

Ends: the tattooed Union member holds Sam's photo. Episode 9 starts there.
Notes: opinion lines "That lie was built to hold", "That was a threat, plain and simple", "That man never even blinked", "He held it in too long", "Wrong boy to pick on". Originals (3): "Yeah. I understand", "Taking a nap? Seriously?", "How come you guys are always so predictable?".
Uncertain: the subtitle names the Union candidate Kang Woo-yeong, same name as Wolf (Woo-yeong) from e07; the script leaves it unnamed. Who exactly Sam fights in the weight room (the "athlete") was read from stills and subtitles only.
