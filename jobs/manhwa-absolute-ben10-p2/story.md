# Absolute Ben 10, Part 2 (chapters 4-6) - story notes
Names: Ben Tennyson, Gwen (Gwendolyn), Kraab (giant armored alien bounty hunter), Aunt Nat. Ben's black cable-armed alien form is not named in the script.
WARNING: the chapters 4-6 panels are the Kraab fight itself, NOT what follows Part 1. There is no Rook in this source. Part 1 already told this fight in compressed form (it ended on Rook only because it added later chapters).
Story told here: Kraab asks Gwen for the Omnitrix, offers a deal, she punches him. Ben (watch timed out, human) lies to Aunt Nat, finds Gwen's phone, Kraab calls him out. Ben turns into the black alien, fight: Kraab electrocutes him, mentions selling humans as meat.
Gwen wakes in a burning cabin (two campers talk about a death-trap cabin), casts a shatter spell and escapes.
Ben offers the watch for Gwen, Kraab says it is bonded and cannot be removed, spots Gwen escaping and aims at her. Ben absorbs Kraab's giant blast and fires it back. Gwen finds Ben, he hides the torn sleeve and watch.
Ended: Kraab's shell sends "transmitting signal". Cliffhanger: whoever hears it comes for Ben.
