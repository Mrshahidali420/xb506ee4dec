# The Lazy Lord Masters the Sword, Part 1 (webtoon eps 1-6; panel sheets only, no english.txt)
Page: Manhwa X, real names. Clips are panel starts (panel N = second 3N); last panel 726 = 2178 s.
Names: Airen Farreira (the Lazy Lord, first son of Baron Farreira, mother dead). Sister Kiril (sorcerer at 11, rides a griffin). Stepmother Baroness Amelia. Butler (unnamed). Old hooded wandering knight = Sir Bran Sommerville (wrote the recommendation). Ahmed (red-haired instructor). Illia Lindsay (white-haired top trainee). Lloyd (blue-haired noble, Count Lloyd's son). Judith (red-haired commoner, ranked 2nd). Karaka (green-haired instructor). Ian (90-year-old head sword master).
Opening: an old villager's diary about a stranger who trained with a sword for 37 years and died kneeling with it (Rema, Laura are the diarist's wife and daughter).
Story: Airen wakes from that dream, can't sleep, grabs a sword, is mocked by guards and nobles, trains daily. Kiril yells at him, mother and Kiril later soften (doll, letter). The old knight sees his willpower and talks the Baron into a letter for Krono Swordsmanship Academy. Airen leaves, runs the entrance test, collapses, ranks 400/400.
Academy: no weapons allowed, only gym and running. Illia fixes his posture, Judith is jealous. A month, then three months of grinding. Ahmed announces a mid-term obstacle course in ten days. Ian, the head sword master, watches Airen lift for 30+ minutes and says leave him alone.
Ending: Airen dreams again, thanks the red-cloaked swordsman, says "I should go now". He wakes with a much bigger body, steam rising. Cliffhanger: what did the dream give him? Part 2 starts with the mid-term evaluation (ten days away).
