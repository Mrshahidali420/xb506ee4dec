Story so far: Jin Runcandel was the weakest swordsman in a clan that lives by survival of the fittest. His brothers broke his sword, the clan threw him out, and a curse called Blade's Delusion kept his talent chained. The god of shadows, Solderet, finally offered him a contract, but armored knights cut him down the same night. He wakes up as a one-year-old at the Rite of Choice, grabs the patriarch's sword Balisada on purpose, and starts a second life with shadow force the clan has never seen. Under Storm Castle he finds Murakhan, the guardian dragon frozen for a thousand years, who now serves him. With Gilly, Luna and a black cat at his side, Jin survives an ambush, climbs through the cadet ranks against Vellop and the Tona twins, takes a border mission against the Kinzelo and a white werewolf, and ends facing Elder Jed with his father's order hanging over him.
