# Part 4 (chapters 16-20), panel number = t/3

Names (real): Jin Runcandel, Vellop, Mesa, Scott, Sember Bill (Jian kingdom heir), Quazito Truka (white werewolf), Murakhan (dragon, black cat), Gilly (maid), Elder Jed Runcandel (eight-star), Kazine Romero, the Tona twins, Anne and Miu (sisters), Bubarre Gaston the Transfigurator, Kinzelo (terror group).

1. Hook: Jin cuts down cadet Kazine in front of the intermediate class, says "call the doctors".
2. Ten-cadet mission on the Jian border to find Sember Bill; Jin suspects his father picked it.
3. Group Two is attacked, blood in the snow, Mesa missing, one attacker by the footprints.
4. Vellop strikes a Kinzelo scout; Jin offers two choices and swears on clan honor; Kinzelo want to crown their own king.
5. Base in Halfmen land, twenty four-star fighters, Mesa taken for propaganda.
6. Jin orders everyone to stay and goes alone: wind net, ambush, fire mage beaten, camp set on fire, Mesa freed from a trapdoor cell.
7. White werewolf Quazito Truka hits Mesa, honor duel; Jin awakens the sword, werewolf blinded and cut down.
8. Shadow force overload; Murakhan's voice pulls him through. Finds Sember Bill (his dead lover was a Kinzelo copy, Bubarre's work).
9. Home: Gilly congratulates, Murakhan (cat) bites Jin, his shadow force jumped to four-star; 11 survivors of overload, 9 died.
10. Siblings doubt Jin beat a werewolf; Tona twins fear him joining the intermediate class. Mother offers the patriarch's room, Jin refuses (a test).
11. Intermediate class, Elder Jed speaks, twins' men try to intimidate; Kazine Romero steps up, Jin cuts him down, "call the doctors".
12. War of ranks tradition; Jin gives his name (13th child).
13. Ends: Jed's aura, Jin says he is fighting Jed, not the twins; Jed: "explain, before I decide to kill you right here" (cliffhanger).
