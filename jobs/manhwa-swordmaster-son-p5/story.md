# Part 5 (chapters 21-25), panel number = t/3

Names (real): Jin Runcandel, Elder Jed Runcandel, Gilly (maid), Murakhan (dragon, black cat), the Tona twins (Jin's brothers), Anne and Miu (sisters, flagbearers), Luna (older sister, white hair) and her old nanny, Alcaro Chanderer (target), Talaris Endorma (Secret Palace ruler), the Secret Palace lady who arrives (captain of the seven swords, Talaris' daughter, called Sera in the script).

1. Hook: Jin blows up the Moonlight Well inn with a shadow-force lightning spell, then plays the frightened survivor.
2. Answer to Jed: Jin says he only attacks people he can beat; Jed laughs and acknowledges his claim.
3. Home: Gilly and Murakhan; the cadet Jin cut down was in his sisters' faction. Flagbearers fight only flagbearers, so the sisters will sabotage his missions; Jin sees a chance to use his power freely.
4. Jed teaches singing stones; a stone explodes, Jin shouts "get down" and dodges; only Luna ever walked away clean before. All three slash stones every afternoon.
5. Luna and her nanny: five years investigating Jin's assassination attempt, no leads; Luna asks to investigate Miu and Anne.
6. Jin trains till he pukes; Jed is impressed (might break Luna's record).
7. Month later, mission lists; Jin's brothers sent to a diplomatic ceremony as punishment.
8. Jin's mission: kill Alcaro Chanderer in lawless Mahmeet (Secret Palace ruler's lover; his own family wants him dead). Jin accepts; brothers panic.
9. Jin disguises himself, Gilly sees him off; Mahmeet bar fight, barkeep points him to the Moonlight Well inn.
10. Alcaro is a spoiled count's son with guards who hate him. No poison, no open kill. Jin decides: shadow force plus lightning = six-star spell.
11. A Secret Palace lady arrives to kill Alcaro too. Lightning Call blows up the inn; Jin survives thanks to a pendant.
12. She finds him, he plays the coward, she helps him up; the locals' kings defy her and she cuts into them.
13. Ends: "Mission complete", but the one hunting the sorcerer just helped him up (cliffhanger).

Part 6 starts with Jin walking away from the ruined inn.
