# Part 6 (chapters 26-30), panel number = t/3

Names (real): Jin Runcandel, Murakhan (dragon, black cat, Jin's contractor), Gilly (maid), Kazine Romero (five-star cadet, sisters' faction, Jin cut off his arm in part 5), Bubarre Gaston (made the Tomb Giant), Bishkel Iveliano (vice chief, Kinzelo Revolutionaries), Jin's siblings Luntia, Ran, Vuigo, Miu and Anne (the faction behind the cadets), Jin's mother (long black hair), Jin's uncle (eyepatch), Mile Haas and Rima Haas plus one more cadet (Miu and Anne's faction, Jin's mission team).

1. Hook: Jin kills the last begging golem in the Zeifl warehouse.
2. Murakhan scatters an orc pack to protect Jin's classmates, then kills a Tomb Giant in dragon form; he decides not to tell Jin. Bubarre mourns the giant, Bishkel names the Dragon of Shadows and plans a Zeifl meeting.
3. Jin returns from Mahmeet; his uncle cheers. Kazine mocks him, Jin challenges him, wins the duel and reveals he is a five-star at 15. Kazine's lady sends her people along on Jin's next mission.
4. Jin tells Gilly he showed off on purpose; banquet and visitors coming in a month.
5. Siblings assign Jin a mission in Kollon (Zeifl land). Mother cancels it and punishes them (Luntia probation, Ran and Vuigo lose a sword). Jin asks to keep the mission; mother promises him Ran and Vuigo's swords if he completes it.
6. Kollon: three spy cadets, Jin drugs their food, goes alone to warehouse three, uses a guard's blood on a magic circle. It turns the two guards into biological golems; he kills them by their cores. The last one begs to die.
7. Ends: Jin still has to take the relic; someone is making humans into monsters.

Part 7 starts with Jin still in the warehouse with the relic unclaimed.
