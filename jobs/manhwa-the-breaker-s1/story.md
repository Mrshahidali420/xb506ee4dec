# The Breaker, chapters 1-72 (complete story, full video)
Names used: Shi-woon Lee (bullied student, hero), Han Chun-woo (substitute teacher, secretly Goomoonryong the Nine Arts Dragon), Changho (bully), Sae-hee (girl Shi-woon likes), Shiho (new health teacher, chases Han), Song (gang boss), Kang-sung (Alliance top fighter), the Murim Alliance and its old chief, the Sunwoo clan, Nine Clans.
1. Shi-woon is beaten daily by Changho's gang. New teacher Han mocks him as a coward and wrecks a gang of thugs at a Muay Thai gym.
2. After more bullying Shi-woon goes up on the roof; Han catches him, the railing breaks, both fall onto the vice principal's car and live.
3. Shi-woon begs to be taught. Han's price: risk your life. Night bridge, Shi-woon jumps, Han pulls him out, training starts (laps, breathing, ki).
4. Gang boss Song hunts the Nine Arts Dragon; Han shows his dragon tattoo and breaks Song's iron hand. Shi-woon stands up to Changho.
5. Murim men in suits and the Nine Clans start hunting the Dragon. Bikers ambush Shi-woon; Han saves him. Sae-hee is kidnapped; Shi-woon rescues her.
6. Years ago Han lost control of his rage and the elders branded him a monster. The Alliance uses Shi-woon as bait and traps Han in a business tower (fire alarm, poison gas).
7. Han turns into a raging monster, wrecks the Alliance fighters and nearly kills Shi-woon. Shi-woon refuses to leave, says "this is not the Sunsengnim I believe in", and punches him; the rage stops.
8. Kang-sung (whose master Han killed) steps in but does not blame him. Han vanishes; Shi-woon keeps calling him with no answer.
9. The Alliance is broken, the Sunwoo clan elders want Shi-woon as heir, suited men show up at his school, he sends them away. END: coward turned fighter, full story closed.
