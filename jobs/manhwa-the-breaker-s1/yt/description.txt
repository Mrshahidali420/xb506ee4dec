The Breaker full story recap: the complete manhwa, chapters 1 to 72, from the first beating to the real ending, in one video.

Shi-woon Lee is the weakest kid at his high school. He gets beaten every day, he has no friends, and one day he walks up to the school roof to end it all. Then a lazy new teacher shows up: Han Chun-woo, a chain-smoking substitute who mocks him for being a coward. What nobody knows is that Han is Goomoonryong, the Nine Arts Dragon, the most feared fighter in the hidden world of the Murim.

Shi-woon begs Han to train him. Han's price is simple: risk your life. What follows is brutal training, street gangs, an iron-fisted boss, the Murim Alliance, the Nine Clans, poison gas on a school roof, and a teacher who has to choose between his student and his past.

- How a bullied coward becomes the student of a martial arts legend
- Han Chun-woo's secret as the Nine Arts Dragon (Goomoonryong)
- Shi-woon's training, his first real fight and the people who try to break him
- The Murim Alliance, Kang-sung, Shiho and the war over the Dragon
- The ending of The Breaker, explained

The Breaker (Korean: 브레이커) is a martial arts manhwa by Jeon Geuk-jin and Park Jin-hwan. If you liked Lookism, Viral Hit, Weak Hero or How to Fight, this is the classic that started the "bullied kid learns to fight" story.
