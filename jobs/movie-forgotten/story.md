# Forgotten (2017), full movie recap, Spoiler Room (fake names)

Names used: Jack (the young man the film calls Jin-seok), Mike (the "brother" Yu-seok), Mom, Dad (the fake family). The doctor, the girl and the boy in the 1997 flashback are unnamed. Music: Lordly_Feder.

Story told: Jack moves into a new house with Mom, Dad and golden-boy brother Mike, hears noises upstairs, nobody else does. One rainy night Mike is shoved into a black van, the plate doesn't exist, the detectives doubt Jack. Mike comes back with no memory, limps on the wrong leg, Jack follows him, gets chased, and is told none of it happened. Mom reports his words on the phone. Jack runs to a police station, says it's 1997, the officer shows the 2017 calendar (he is 41). The people who took him explain: a family was murdered on 20 Dec 1997, case went cold, they found Jack, he claimed no memory, a hypnosis expert put him back in 1997 with an actor family in a replica of the murder house, waiting for rain to restage the night. It wore off, he escaped by jumping from a car, ended in surgery. Flashback: broke 21-year-old, brother bedridden, a chat-room offer to kill for money, "only get rid of the woman", he told the boy to hide and count, the woman and girl died. In hospital a man in a black suit stands over him with a syringe and asks why he didn't kill that kid too. Then the suit man lies dead on the pavement. Last shots: sunny lake and a little boy.

Ended: closing line "Some nights you can never forget. Wonder what we spoil next?" (movie, no sequel hook).

Uncertain bits (no english.txt, read off the shot sheets): who the man in the black suit really is (not named, only his subtitled lines are used), and who put him on the pavement (narration only says he is lying dead). Whether the police station was real is left open.
