# I Saw the Devil (2010) - recap notes (Spoiler Room, movie, whole story in one video)

Names (fake, keep for any follow-up): Daniel = Soo-hyun, the secret agent. Emma = Ju-yeon, his fiancee. Victor = Kyung-chul, the serial killer. Chief Brown = Emma's father, retired police chief. Anna = Se-yeon, Emma's sister. Ross = Daniel's boss and friend, the one who knows about the tracker. Mark = Tae-joo, Victor's killer friend.

Story told, in order:
- Hook: Daniel catches Victor and lets him go. Then back to the snowy night Emma's car breaks down and Victor "helps".
- Emma is murdered. Chief Brown finds her. Funeral, Daniel promises to make Victor pay.
- Daniel takes two weeks off, takes a GPS and microphone capsule, gets the police files (four suspects).
- First suspect beaten into confessing. Victor found through his parents (insurance trick).
- Greenhouse fight: Daniel beats Victor, makes him swallow the capsule, lets him go.
- Victor hurts a taxi killer pair, hits a clinic and threatens a nurse, Daniel arrives and beats him again, "your nightmare is only getting worse".
- Chief Brown and Anna beg Daniel to stop. Victor hides at Mark's house, Daniel listens through the capsule and breaks in. Brutal fight, both knocked out for two days.
- Victor phones Daniel, says Emma was pregnant and begged. Victor says he will surrender but first he goes after Emma's family. Mark (in hospital) warns Daniel. Daniel is too late, police blame him.
- Victor gives himself up, Daniel takes him from the police. Quiet room: Daniel says he will kill him at the most painful moment. Victor begs, then refuses to break.
- Victor's own family comes to the door, Victor screams not to open it. Daniel walks away and cries. Ends on "why does it feel like he lost".
