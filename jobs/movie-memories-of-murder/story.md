# Memories of Murder (2003), full movie recap (Spoiler Room)

What happened: hook is Ryan's gun on a man in the tunnel, then the DNA papers from America say no match. Flashback to 1986: girl's body in a drain in the rice fields, kids and a tractor ruin the scene. Jack trusts his eyes, beats Tommy into a confession (footprint, woods re-enactment). Ryan arrives, reads files in a blackout, the press re-enactment collapses when Tommy's father screams he is innocent. Ryan's rain/red pattern, third body, dinner fight, rainy-night bait plan with Anna fails. Sad Letter radio clue, saunas, shaman. Sam caught at a quarry and broken into a false confession; Ryan finds a survivor (soft hands). Another murder while Sam is in a cell. Postcard leads to Peter (soft hands, no proof). Tape shows Tommy talked like a witness; Tommy sees Peter's photo, ends up on the train tracks. DNA goes to America, Mike loses a leg, Peter slips his watchers, another girl is killed in rain. Ryan beats Peter in a train tunnel with a gun; DNA papers say no match; Jack lets him go.
