# Miracle in Cell No. 7 (2013), Spoiler Room, full movie recap

Names used (fake): Sam = Yong-gu (slow-minded loving father), Lily = Ye-seung (his little girl), Amy = Ji-young (the dead girl), Chief = the police commissioner (her father), Hale = the prison head guard.

Story told: Sam and Lily try to buy a Sailor Moon bag. Sam sees Amy with the same bag, follows her, she falls and hits her head, he gives her CPR, her mother screams kidnapper. Amy is the police chief's daughter, so the cops beat Sam into a confession while Lily waits alone and then screams after the police van.
In prison (Cell Seven) the cellmates first attack him, then see he is gentle, and smuggle Lily in inside a box. They hide her from the guards. Hale catches them, then a fire breaks out; Sam gives Hale CPR and saves him, so Hale looks the other way and Lily even goes to school.
The chief sends a prosecutor who threatens Lily, so Sam signs the confession and says yes in court. He is sentenced to death. The cellmates stage a last day: a balloon ride over the yard, a birthday seaweed soup. Guards come for Sam, Lily begs at the bars, he tells her not to cry.
Years later Lily is a lawyer, proves the old statements were forged, and the court clears his name. Ends with Lily in the snow remembering the balloon.

Hook = the guards dragging Sam from Lily (clips 7031-7050), replayed at its real place near the end. Two orig lines (the farewell at 7056, the van at 997).
