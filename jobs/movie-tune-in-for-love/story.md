# Tune in for Love (2019), spoiler page

Fake names: Mia (Mi-su, bakery girl, later a radio writer), Henry (Hyun-woo, fresh out of juvenile detention, later a camera operator), Ruby (Eun-ja, Mia's best friend, later runs a noodle restaurant). No other names are used.

What happened (follows the film, which jumps between years):
- Hook: Mia runs across the city.
- 1994: Henry walks into Misu Bakery asking for soybean food, Ruby guesses he just left jail (he did, a juvenile detention center where a radio show kept him going). He takes the part-time job, learns dough, grows close to Mia. At Christmas his old crew drag him to a bar, the night goes wrong, he disappears, the bakery closes.
- 1997: Mia, a college student, gets a two-month radio job. She meets Henry again in the street, he joins the army the next day. They spend the night; he promises to email.
- 2000: Emails dry up. The old bakery is torn down. Henry back in the city, works at a gym, chats online, calls. Money goes missing at his job, he is blamed, goes silent. Mia hurts.
- Years later: Mia is a radio writer, Henry answers an ad for a camera operator on a visual radio show. They find each other, cook, read, argue about his past ("I wish you had told me", "I always prayed to God to let me live a normal life"). His old crew pulls him into a garage fight. Mia drives away, he runs after her car and holds her, then she leaves again.
- Ending: Mia hears the radio show ask "whose name comes to mind", runs across the city to the station. Henry stands behind a camera, they see each other through the glass and smile.

Uncertain parts: the film cuts between years without clear labels and has no english.txt, so the middle (2000 job, money, garage fight) was read from burned-in subtitles and stills. Orig lines (3): "I wish you had told me.", "I always prayed to God to let me live a normal life.", "I love you, Mia." (subtitle says Mi-su).
Opinion lines: "Silence hurts more than a goodbye", "Two people, one phone call apart", "She never stopped waiting for him".
