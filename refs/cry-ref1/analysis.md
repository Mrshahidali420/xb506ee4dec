### Conversion study

- Compression: about 7 minutes of original footage (market 33 s, test 13 s, restaurant 60 s, fire 5 s, toys 29 s, name + sword + bench + walk-away + hug about 3 min, Danny school and noodle shop 65 s) became 115 s of clip, about 4:1 on the footage taken. Against the story span they stand in (E2 10:14 to 61:23, 51 minutes) it is roughly 27:1.
- Skipped between beats: fire to toys about 6 min (investigation, hospital, the mother); toys to the name scene about 31 min (the conspiracy thread, prosecutors, the gold-collection campaign, Dol Mok's days in the house); hug to Danny's school about 7 min. The whole Heroic Corps / General Baek San plot, the detective and the prosecutor are gone; the clip tells it as a family story.
- Reordered: in the original the genius-boy scenes (E2 53:17-55:52) come AFTER the orphanage flashback, as the present day. The clip puts them first (0:00-0:45) and tells the flashback as backstory after one reveal line. The fire and toy shots (E2 10-17 min) are pulled forward into the 0:50-0:57 reveal. Order inside the flashback (name, sword, bench, leave, run back, hug) is kept as it was.
- Exaggerated or changed by the narration: "perfect score on every test" (one test is shown); "photographic memory" (shown as quick multiplying plus reciting a menu); "the dad has never told his wife who Ethan really is" (E2 26:30 has him telling her the boy is "my friend's son", so she knows part of it); "six years" is the narrator's figure; "In this child's heart, this man is already his real dad" is added feeling, not a drama line; "Don't get sick" is the one original line kept near verbatim.
- Invented or glued: the family-money explanation is laid over the sword-play scene, which has no money talk; from 1:42 on, scenes far apart in the story are joined into one "past six years" montage.
- Renamed: Soo Yeon (real name), Dol Mok = Ethan, Min Jae = Danny; the dub renames the cover name too, so "Ethan" keeps the meaning "the safe name".
- Music vs peak: the original's peak is the run-back and hug (E2 52:29-52:58, clip 1:30-1:39) after the wall cry (51:37-52:06, clip 1:21-1:27). music.txt puts The Rain at 0:50-1:20: it starts at the reveal, runs through the name, bench, walk-away and wall cry, and ends about 10 s before the hug. That does not fit the earlier note that it "holds through the hug"; the windows are 30 s Shazam samples, so the true end is unmeasured and needs a stem check. The second block (2:30-3:00) sits on the piggy bank and the overheard argument, quiet kindness beats that are not in episodes 1-2.
- One line: a loss arc of about 4 minutes of story was kept in order, its setup moved to the front, and 51 minutes of plot around it erased.
