## Emotion mechanics (why it works)
1. The ability has a cost, told in the first 20 s ("serious price"); the viewer waits for the cost to bite.
2. Dramatic irony: the narrator tells us "Jack has done so much for Jenny behind the scenes" and the next shot is him publicly scolding her. We know the warmth she does not.
3. Justice payoff at 1:02-1:15: the sneering ex-chief is caught in three short lines. This is the laugh and the cheer of the clip.
4. Setup and payoff by prop: the air freshener. Reaction shots of Jenny sniffing the air carry the comedy.
5. Cut to the scar on the last frame, a callback to the opening vision.
